Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

What does the 7-day live arrival guarantee cover?

If any live fish arrives deceased or shows severe health issues within 7 days, contact care@fish-keeper.com with clear photos and your order number. We will refund or replace — no need to return the fish.

How is live fish shipping handled in extreme weather?

We use insulated boxes with heat packs in winter and ice packs in summer. When local temperatures fall outside 2–32°C, we may delay shipping and notify you to reschedule for the safest window.

Can I visit the showroom to pick fish in person?

Yes — our Shanghai showroom is open daily. You can browse 40+ display tanks and our advisors will help you select compatible stock. Reservations are recommended on weekends.

Do you offer aquascaping consulting?

Yes. We provide both online layout advice and on-site aquascaping service in selected cities. Tick "Aquascaping consulting" in the form above and describe your tank for a tailored proposal.
